# Add-ons: making it interactive and fun

## High impact (build these)
1. **🎤 Audience texts the bot live.** A big QR code: "Scan to message the salon's new AI assistant." A live counter climbs on the projector as the room texts it.
2. **🔥 "Roast My Website" mode.** Toggle for a savage, funny audit: *"Your contact form collects phone numbers and then… does nothing with them. Bold strategy."*
3. **⏱️ Before/after time-lapse.** Split screen replays "a Saturday at the salon": messages pile up for the owner on the left, the agent answers each in seconds on the right, with a ₹ counter ticking up.
4. **🗣️ Hinglish support.** The agent understands and replies in Hinglish ("Hair spa kitne ka hai?").
5. **🚨 Live human handoff.** An angry complaint comes in; the agent de-escalates, refuses to promise a refund, and the owner's phone gets an alert.

## Medium effort
6. **Side-by-side competitor scan:** two salons in the same area; who loses customers to slow replies.
7. **Chat-export insights:** upload a WhatsApp export and chart the top repeat questions.
8. **Shareable score card:** Lighthouse-style image ("My business scored 34/100 🙈").
9. **Guardrail panel:** what the agent refused to answer and why.

## Stretch
10. **📞 Voice agent:** same agent answers a phone call via Vapi or Retell.
11. **Response-time check:** with the owner's permission only, send a test enquiry and measure human reply time.
