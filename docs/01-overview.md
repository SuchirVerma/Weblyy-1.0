# Overview

## One-liner
Paste your business URL. We find the customer conversations your team handles by hand and turn the top ones into working AI agents in minutes.

## Problem
Small businesses spend hours every week answering the same WhatsApp messages, emails and form submissions: prices, availability, bookings, follow-ups. They know they "should use AI" but don't know where to start or what's safe to automate.

## Solution
1. **Scan:** crawl the website and map every customer touchpoint.
2. **Ask:** 5 quick follow-up questions, or an optional WhatsApp chat or inbox export, show the manual work behind those touchpoints.
3. **Score:** each workflow gets an automation score based on volume, repetitiveness and risk, plus estimated hours saved per week.
4. **Deploy:** top workflows become importable n8n blueprints, and one agent goes live immediately, trained on the business's own site content.

## What's different
- **URL-first, not a questionnaire.** The owner gets insight within 60 seconds, before typing anything.
- **Multi-channel.** WhatsApp, email and forms in one map; most tools look at one channel.
- **Ends with a running agent,** not just a PDF audit.
- **Honest scoring.** We flag what *shouldn't* be automated (complaints, refunds, anything sensitive).

## What we deliberately don't claim
We don't say we find every internal process from a website. We start with what's visible and use the owner's input for the rest.

## Summary
Small businesses spend hours answering the same WhatsApp messages, emails and form submissions by hand, and they don't know what's safe to automate. Our platform takes a business website URL and maps every customer touchpoint: WhatsApp links, forms, emails, booking widgets and FAQs. It then asks a few quick follow-up questions to find the manual work behind them. Each workflow gets an automation score and an estimate of hours saved. Anything sensitive, like complaints or refunds, is flagged as human-only. The top workflows become ready-to-import n8n blueprints, and one AI agent goes live right away, trained on the business's own website content.
